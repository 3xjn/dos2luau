# Third-party resources

The CP437 bitmap in `runtime/CP437Font.luau` is the Oldschool BIOS 8×8 font
by VileR, converted to CP437 bitmap order by Susam Pal's PC Face project.
The bitmap is redistributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International**, including its attribution and license reference in the
source. Changes here are its representation as a Luau buffer and embedding.

- Source: https://github.com/susam/pcface/tree/main/out/oldschool-bios-8x8
- Font author: https://int10h.org/oldschool-pc-fonts/
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode
- PC Face licensing: https://github.com/susam/pcface#license

The standard VGA palette in `runtime/Video.luau` comes from the rust-osdev VGA
project. The complete MIT permission and warranty notice is retained in that
module.

- Source: https://github.com/rust-osdev/vga/blob/master/src/colors.rs
- Copyright: Philipp Oppermann, Gerd Zellweger, and the libcpu developers.

Optional downloaded games remain in ignored local directories and are not part
of the source distribution. Their original copyrights remain with their owners.
The included demo program and square-wave sample are generated by
`scripts/build-demo.ts` and do not contain downloaded game assets.

The LZEXE layout, decompression algorithm, and depacker signature references in
`compiler/src/lzexe.ts` are based on UNLZEXE 0.8 by Kou, David Kirschbaum,
Alan Modra, and Vesselin Bontchev. The UNLZEXE companion is described as public
domain by its distributor. The compiler contains a bounded TypeScript port;
the original LZEXE packer is separate software.

- Publisher: https://bellard.org/lzexe/
- Distribution documentation: https://pushbx.org/ecm/doc/lzexe.htm
